# Historical incident (synthetic example)

**Summary:** For about 20 minutes one processor answered slowly. Several authorizations timed out although the processor had executed them.

**Impact:** 14 payments were UNCERTAIN for up to 3 minutes. No customer was charged twice because retries were not sent.

**Resolution:** The uncertain-payment resolver looked up each request id at the processor and moved the payments to AUTHORIZED.

**Lessons:** Timeouts are ambiguous. Resolve by lookup, not by resending.
